Privacy Policy ​

Effective Date: April 2026

AskBot AI Custom Widget, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy details the types of information handled when you use our custom widgets within SAP Analytics Cloud.

1. Data Collection and Usage ​

Our custom widgets execute purely as client-side JavaScript extensions within your SAP Analytics Cloud environment.

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not track, collect, or store any personal data, business analytics data, or proprietary reports.
  • Local Browser Environment: All logic runs securely on your machine (the user's browser).

2. Third-Party Interactions ​

To provide features like automatic update notifications, the widget occasionally makes direct network requests from your browser:

  • Version Checks (GitHub API): The widget checks the official GitHub repository for new releases. This only transmits your browser's standard network headers (such as IP address) directly to GitHub.

Please review GitHub's Privacy Statement for details on how they process these network requests.

TIP

To suppress automatic update notifications, navigate to the Custom Widget Builder panel, open Advanced Settings, and deactivate the corresponding toggle option.

3. Data Security ​

Because no user data is processed on external servers operated by us, there is no risk of data breaches on our end regarding your company's SAP data. Your data remains fully bound by your organization's SAP Analytics Cloud security and network setup.

4. Policy Changes ​

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features or functionality. The latest version will always be available on this page.

If you have any inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out via GitHub or LinkedIn.
